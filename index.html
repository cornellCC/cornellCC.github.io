01 / Our perspective+

The organization
behind the advice.

Consulting clubs help their clients solve complex problems. But who helps the clubs navigate their own?

Cornell Consulting Consulting Club brings a focused perspective to student consulting organizations. From recruiting your next class to handing over the presidency, we help turn good intentions into systems that last.

How we approach the work ↗
02 / Our practices+

A stronger club.
At every level.

Practical support for the decisions, people, and processes that shape your organization.

01

Organizational strategy

+

Give your organization a clear direction—and a structure that can carry it forward.

  • Mission and positioning
  • Leadership and governance
  • Succession and knowledge transfer
02

Recruitment & selection

+

Build a thoughtful recruiting process that connects your club with the people who will move it forward.

  • Candidate experience
  • Interview structure and evaluation
  • New member onboarding
03

Analyst development

+

Translate individual potential into consistent, confident project work.

  • Training curriculum design
  • Mentorship and feedback
  • Case and project preparation
04

Engagement operations

+

Create the conditions for teams to do their best work, from the first client conversation to the final handoff.

  • Client sourcing and project scoping
  • Delivery cadence and quality review
  • Engagement retrospectives

03 / The way we work

Built with you.
Designed to outlast us.

Useful recommendations become lasting progress when the people doing the work can put them into practice.

01

Understand

Listen to your leadership and members. Map what works, what gets in the way, and what matters next.

02

Design

Translate the diagnosis into a focused plan, with clear priorities and tools suited to your club.

03

Put into practice

Work through the details together. Test the approach and refine it around real feedback.

04

Carry forward

Document the decisions, equip the next leaders, and make continuity part of the solution.
