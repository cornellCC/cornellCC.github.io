Working together

How an engagement takes shape

Scope, timing, and responsibilities are agreed together around a defined organizational question.

  1. Define the question

    Establish the decision to be made, the people involved, and the constraints.

  2. Investigate and develop

    Review evidence and existing processes, then work through possible approaches.

  3. Review and hand over

    Discuss recommendations, document the tools, and identify who will maintain them.
