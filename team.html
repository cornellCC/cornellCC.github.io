Co-President

Ellie

Why CCCC?

I’m a sophomore in CAS studying Government. If there is one thing I am passionate about, it is consulting. If there is another, it is consulting about consulting. CCCC combines these passions by providing me with the opportunity to consult with consultants about consulting, while simultaneously consulting on how consulting can be better consulted. Through CCCC, I hope to leverage my consulting expertise to advise aspiring consultants on the complexities of consulting about consulting, ultimately advancing consulting as both a profession and a field of consultative consultation.

Co-President

Micah

Why CCCC?

I’m a sophomore studying Mathematics, with a passion for economics, philosophy, computer science, and above all, consulting, a field I believe is ripe for consulting-driven disruption.

I’m driven to leverage consulting to redefine environmental consulting, synergizing best-in-class consulting frameworks to deliver consulting solutions that move the needle on consulting itself.

As a thought leader in the consulting-about-consulting space, I take a holistic, consulting-first approach to ideating scalable consulting strategies that empower stakeholders to consult on their consulting journeys.

Through Cornell Consulting Consulting Club, I’ve circled back on countless consulting engagements, consulting with consultants on how to consult more consultatively and unlock the full value-add of the consulting ecosystem.

I’m passionate about building a consulting pipeline that bridges the gap between consulting and consulting, because at the end of the day, consulting isn’t just what I do; consulting is who I am.
