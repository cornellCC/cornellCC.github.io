Co-President

Eliana

Why CCCC?

I’m a sophomore in CAS studying Economics with a deep passion for business, problem-solving, and most of all, consulting.

The Cornell Consulting Consulting Club empowers me to consult with fellow aspiring consultants about consulting, gain consulting experience through consulting-related consulting opportunities, and ultimately become a consultant who knows how to consult about consulting.

Co-President

Ellie

Why CCCC?

I’m a sophomore in CAS studying Government. If there is one thing I am passionate about, it is consulting. If there is another, it is consulting about consulting. CCCC combines these passions by providing me with the opportunity to consult with consultants about consulting, while simultaneously consulting on how consulting can be better consulted. Through CCCC, I hope to leverage my consulting expertise to advise aspiring consultants on the complexities of consulting about consulting, ultimately advancing consulting as both a profession and a field of consultative consultation.

Co-President

Brenna

Why CCCC?

I am a sophomore studying history and government, and I have harbored a passion for consulting since I was born. I always dreamed of pursuing a career as a consultant in consulting, and I have found a love for consulting about consulting through Cornell Consulting Consulting Club. As co-president, CCCC has given me the incredible opportunity to be consulted by consulting consultants regarding my passions, and I look forward to spreading my consulting knowledge through consulting future consulting consultants on consulting.
