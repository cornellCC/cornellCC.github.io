Analyst development

Education connected to the work.

Our practices

Organizational strategyRecruitment & selectionAnalyst developmentEngagement operations

A useful training program helps members practice the skills their projects require. We connect learning objectives, exercises, and feedback to the realities of student consulting.

Questions we address

What should a new analyst be able to do? How does training connect to project assignments? Where do members need more practice or feedback?

The work

Assess the existing curriculum, identify gaps, design applied exercises, and define a feedback cadence that project leaders can sustain.

Illustrative deliverables

A learning progression, session outlines, practice assignments, feedback templates, and a mentor discussion guide.

Deliverables are examples of potential scope, not a record of completed client engagements.
