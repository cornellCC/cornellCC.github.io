The work you would do

Our work concerns how consulting clubs operate. It calls for careful research, structured thinking, and an interest in the people who will use the recommendations.

Projects may involve reviewing a training curriculum, mapping a recruiting process, or developing a leadership transition guide. The goal is a practical output that a club can maintain.

Ways to prepare

  • Think about a student organization you know. What works well, and what repeatedly creates friction?
  • Practice explaining a problem before proposing a solution.
  • Consider what evidence would change your recommendation.
