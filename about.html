Why consulting clubs?

Student consulting clubs bring together ambitious people to solve problems for external clients. Their internal challenges are often just as demanding: limited time, uneven experience, changing leadership, and knowledge that leaves when members graduate.

Cornell Consulting Consulting Club exists to give those challenges sustained attention. We advise on the structures and routines that help a club operate well across academic years.

Our approach

We start by understanding how a club actually works. That means examining existing materials, listening to members and leaders, and agreeing on a specific problem before proposing a solution.

The result should be something the organization can use: a clearer decision process, a more coherent training program, or a handover that preserves essential knowledge.

Working principles

Fit the organization
A process should match the club’s size, time, and priorities.
Make the reasoning visible
Recommendations should make their assumptions and tradeoffs clear.
Plan for continuity
Documentation and ownership belong in the work from the beginning.
